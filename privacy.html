ASTRO NOVA – Privacy Policy

Developer: Double Trouble Studios · Last updated: 3 November 2026

ASTRO NOVA is a single-player and 2-player arcade game. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not show ads, and we do not use analytics or tracking. This page explains the little data the game does handle.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (Cores, upgrades, unlocked ships, records, achievements and settings) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it. You can export or import a save file yourself from the Settings screen.

2. Online co-op (only when you use it)

The game connects to the internet only when you choose Co-op → Host game or Join game. Then:

  • Connection setup. To pair the two players with a 6-digit room code, the game sends technical connection data (your device's network/IP addresses and supported video formats) through the free public PeerJS service (peerjs.com) and asks a public STUN server (Google stun.l.google.com, Cloudflare stun.cloudflare.com) to find a network route. These services only see this connection data, not your game.
  • The game stream. After pairing, the game picture, sound and controls flow directly between the two players' devices (peer-to-peer). Because of this, the other player's device can see your IP address, as with any direct connection.
  • Player name. The co-op name you type is shown to the other player. Use a nickname, not your real name.

You can turn off Play online in Co-op settings; then co-op works only on your own local network and none of the services above are contacted.

3. What we do not do

  • No account, sign-in, e-mail or phone number is collected.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no analytics, no crash-report SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files are accessed.
  • No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

4. Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Co-op is optional and shows only the nickname a player types.

5. Permissions

The Android app requests only the Internet permission, used for online co-op as described above.

6. Changes and contact
