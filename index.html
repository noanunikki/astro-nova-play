How to play

Survive as long as you can. At 12:00 the Overlord arrives.

Move & Aim

WASD or arrow keys to move. Your ship fires automatically toward the mouse cursor. Gamepad: left stick move, right stick aim.

Dash

Space / Shift dashes in your movement direction and makes you invulnerable for a moment. Dash through bullets and enemy rings.

Overload & Ability

Kills charge the Overload bar: E or Right-click unleashes a screen-clearing shockwave. Q fires your ship's unique ability on a cooldown.

Level up & evolve

Collect blue gems to level up and choose 1 of 3 upgrades (123). Max a weapon and meet its passive requirement to unlock a gold EVOLUTION. Reroll with R.

Combo

Chain kills quickly to build a score multiplier. Getting hit halves your combo.

Bosses & pickups

A boss appears every 3 minutes and drops a chest of epic upgrades. Watch for red warning zones. Pick up green repair, blue magnet and orange nuke crystals.

Progression

Every run earns Cores. Spend them on permanent upgrades and unlock new ships with different weapons and stats.

Shrines & arenas

Touch magenta dark shrines for risky pacts. Pillars block bullets, Reactor zones burn, Collapse shrinks the arena.

Touch controls

Left thumb: floating move stick. Right thumb: aim stick (the ship keeps pointing where you last aimed; Settings can switch to follow-movement or auto-aim). Buttons: DASH, SKILL (ship ability) and OVERLOAD. Play in landscape; use "Add to Home Screen" for a full-screen app.

Other

Esc / P pauses. Enemy bullets glow red. Yellow lines mean a dasher is about to charge. Green circles are bombs.
